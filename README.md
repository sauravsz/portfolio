## 📊 Field Research: Amul FMCG Distribution

- **Scope:** 2 months of direct retail market observation across 140+ retail stores in Silchar.
- **Key Findings:**
  - **Asymmetric Margins:** Traditional mom-and-pop stores operate on thin 8–10% gross margins on staple dairy, requiring high turnover (>3x weekly).
  - **Lead-Time Friction:** Local walk-in replenishments take 0–1 days but carry retail premiums, while distributor delivery takes 2–4 days, causing stockouts during demand spikes.
  - **Tool Motivation:** Built automated depletion models to calculate exact "Order-By" cutoff dates before stockouts occur.
