How I Work With AI

AI is great for drafting and sorting information, but it struggles with real-world messiness. Here is how I combine AI with practical work:

01

Go to the Source

Don't just read summaries online. Spend time with real store owners, distributors, and delivery drivers to see where bottlenecks actually happen.

Real field observation
02

Keep the Small Details

Real supply chains break on small things: supplier order cutoffs, late delivery trucks, or thin store profit margins. Don't smooth those details away.

Real constraints matter
03

Math for Numbers, AI for Words

Never ask an AI to do math. Use AI to understand messy text or voice notes, and use clear math formulas to calculate the numbers reliably.

Accurate calculations
