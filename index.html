Escaping the AI Average

Because LLMs calculate the mathematical average of documents, relying on generic AI prompts homogenizes thinking. To build high-value operational tools, I focus on the three moats AI cannot replicate:

01

Move Upstream

AI commoditizes downstream summarization. True operational alpha lives upstream: capturing firsthand friction in retail beats, talking to distributors, and observing unasked questions in physical networks.

First-person tacit observation
02

Hunt Down Details

Summarization is fundamentally the deletion of details. But supply chains succeed or fail on granular edge cases: asymmetric lead-time cutoffs, bottle-cap stockout penalties, and PTR retail margins.

Preserving constraint nuances
03

Deterministic Solvers

Never let an LLM do supply chain math. Use fast LLMs (Groq) to translate unstructured human text and voice into structured parameters, then pass those matrices to deterministic Linear Programming solvers.

Zero-hallucination math
